GEMINIDS know more
Autonomous meteor astrometry station. Self-built, closed-loop, unattended.
"The instrument is the means; the measurement is the point."
Research question: does the measured radiant position and activity rate of the 2026 Geminids match the values predicted by the International Meteor Organization?
Design principle: no purchased GoTo hardware, no third-party plate-solving service.
Heritage-90 OTA on an AS5600-encoded AZ mount, ESP8266 PID controller, Pi HQ camera. Closed-loop pointing, blind plate-solving, real-time frame-difference meteor detection.
Doubles as a mini observatory: the 90mm OTA handles deep-space astrophotography with fine tracking and auto-stacking, while a separate fixed wide-field camera runs meteor detection all night.
Per-session meteor log, radiant convergence, and measured-vs-predicted ZHR curve — compiled and published without manual intervention.
Open parts list, open-source code — built to be reproducible, and extensible to multi-station triangulation.
Request a deep-sky target and the station will slew to it at the right time, image it, stack it, and send the result back to you.
